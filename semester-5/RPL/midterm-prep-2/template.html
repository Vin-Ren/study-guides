ModelWhat a complete answer showsCommon reason to revise
Use caseSystem boundary, external actor roles, actor goals, correct include/extend or precondition meaning, and a specification for any requested flow. [L06 pp. 10–20, 36–49]A UI step becomes a use case; an offline action sits inside the boundary; optional work becomes mandatory.
DFDVerb phrase processes, noun flows/stores, valid directions, each process with input and output, matching parent/child boundary flows. [L07 pp. 8–11, 35–38]A direct entity-to-store flow, a two-headed arrow, a missing output, or an unbalanced child diagram.
Class diagramOnly facts and links the system must retain, attributes on their owning class or relationship, role names and multiplicities at the correct ends. [L08 pp. 23–34, 41–47]Duplicate attributes, invented stored links, wrong end of multiplicity, or a global flag for a relationship-specific fact.
DesignCRC and diagram agreement, cohesive responsibility, limited coupling, valid contract pre/post/invariant, and no avoidable message chain. [L09 pp. 10–12; L10 pp. 6–23, 38–43]A collaborator is absent from the class model, a method needs strangers' internals, or a contract promise cannot be checked.
